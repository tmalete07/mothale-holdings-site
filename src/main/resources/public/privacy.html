Privacy Policy

How we collect, use and protect your personal information.

Last updated: 10 October 2026

1. Who we are

Mothale Holdings (Pty) Ltd (Enterprise No. 2026/503839/07), 1 Bokmakierie Road, Daggafontein, Springs, Gauteng, 1559, is the responsible party for the personal information collected through this website, as defined in the Protection of Personal Information Act, 2013 (POPIA).

2. What information we collect

We only collect the information you give us through the forms on this website:

  • Rent a Bike enquiry: full name, email address, cell number, preferred collection date (optional) and the address your quote should be sent to.
  • Contact form: name, email address, phone number (optional) and your message.

3. Why we use your information

  • To prepare and send you a scooter rental quote.
  • To reply to questions you send us.
  • To send you a confirmation email when you submit an enquiry.
  • To send you one follow-up email about 30 days after your quote expires, if you have not accepted it. We will not send any further follow-ups after that.

We do not use your information for any other purpose, and we never sell it.

4. Your consent

Before you submit a form, you must tick a box to agree to this policy. If you do not agree, please do not submit the form. You may withdraw your consent at any time by emailing us.

5. Who we share it with

Your information is only seen by Mothale Holdings staff and directors who handle enquiries. We use an email service provider to send emails, which processes your details only for that purpose. We do not share your information with anyone else unless the law requires us to.

6. How long we keep it

We keep enquiry and contact information for approximately 12 months from your last interaction with us (enquiry, quote or follow-up). After that, it is archived or deleted.

7. How we protect it

Information sent through this website is encrypted using HTTPS. Access to stored information is limited to authorised people only.

8. Your rights

Under POPIA, you have the right to:

  • Ask what personal information we hold about you.
  • Ask us to correct or update your information.
  • Ask us to delete your information.
  • Object to us using your information.

To make a request, email us at mothalestockvel@gmail.com.

9. Complaints

If you are unhappy with how we handle your information, please contact us first. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Information Regulator of South Africa: inforegulator.org.za.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be on this page, with the date it was last updated.
