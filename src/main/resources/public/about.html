Our company

Mothale Holdings (Pty) Ltd is a South African company based in Springs, Gauteng. We rent scooters to individuals who deliver for platforms such as Uber Eats and Mr D.

Every rental is covered by a formal Scooter Rental Agreement, so drivers know exactly what is expected on both sides.
